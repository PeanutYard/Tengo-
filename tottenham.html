Learn about Kevin Danso's Successful Time at Spurs

Transfer Details

Kevin Danso moved to Spurs in the 2024/2025 season in January following a huge centre back injury crisis within the club. The deal was a loan with an obligation to to buy for 6 months with a deal in place worth 25 million pounds. Despite being a backup for Cristian Romero that season, he gave 100% in every game he played for Spurs. Becoming a huge part of the winning the Europa League.
